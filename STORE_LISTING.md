## Full Description

**Transform your daily routine and build lasting habits with Aimly!**

Aimly is your clean, intuitive, and privacy-focused daily goal tracker, habit builder, and reminder companion. Whether you want to drink more water, exercise daily, read books, study, or take daily medication on time, Aimly empowers you to achieve what matters most every single day.

---

### 🔥 Key Features

- **🎯 Personal Daily Goals & Habits**
  Create custom goals or choose from pre-configured habit categories (Water, Exercise, Reading, Study, Medicine, Work, and Custom).

- **⏰ Smart Local Reminders**
  Set precise time-of-day reminders and custom repeat schedules (Everyday, Weekdays, Weekends, or specific days). Reminders continue working reliably when your phone is locked, minimized, or restarted.

- **🔥 Habit Streaks & Visual Progress**
  Track your daily completion streak badges and watch your circular progress rings fill up as you accomplish goals.

- **💤 Lock Screen Snooze & Quick Mark Done**
  Complete goals or snooze reminders directly from your notification bar without opening the app.

- **📊 Comprehensive Statistics & History Log**
  Review your daily, weekly, and monthly goal completion history in detailed calendar views and charts.

- **☁️ Remote Updates & Challenges**
  Stay motivated with optional server updates, weekly habit challenges, and inspirational announcements via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

- **🔒 Offline-First & Private**
  Your core goal tracking works completely offline. Your data stays safely on your device.
