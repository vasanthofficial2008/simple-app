# Privacy Policy for Aimly

**Effective Date**: September 19, 2026

Welcome to **Aimly** ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how Aimly collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Local User Data (Stored on Device)
- **Goals & Habits**: Title, description, target counts, reminder times, category choices, and repeat schedules.
- **Progress & Streaks**: Completion logs, daily history, streak calculations, and app preferences (theme, snooze duration).
- **Storage**: All goal and habit data is stored locally on your device using Android Room Database and SharedPreferences. It is not transmitted to external advertising networks.

### B. Remote Push Notifications & Firebase Data
- **Push Registration Token**: When push notifications are enabled, Aimly generates an anonymous Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration token to deliver server-initiated announcements, weekly motivation messages, and product updates.
- **Device & System Metadata**: Technical information such as operating system version, device model, and app version may be processed by Firebase Cloud Messaging for notification delivery.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely to:
- Schedule and trigger local goal reminders.
- Calculate progress percentages, statistics, and daily streaks.
- Deliver optional push notifications, product updates, and weekly challenges via FCM.
- Improve application performance and stability.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services

Aimly integrates with Google Firebase for remote notifications:
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**: Managed by Google LLC. Please review the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:
- Local database entries are stored in private app storage protected by Android OS sandbox permissions.
- Remote push communication with Firebase is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.

---

## 5. User Control & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data:
- **Delete Goals**: You can delete individual goals or clear all goal data at any time via **Settings > Reset All App Data**.
- **Disable Notifications**: You can toggle global notifications off at any time in **Settings > Notification Preferences** or through Android System Settings.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Aimly is a general productivity and habit-tracking application. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
